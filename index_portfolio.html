01
DataSense AI — Data Analytics Platform
A no-code platform that cleans uploaded data, finds anomalies, segments customers and explains results in plain language. It auto-fixed 8,556 missing values and 2,242 outliers across 2,500+ records, lifting the data quality score to 92/100. An Isolation Forest and K-Means pipeline detected 117 anomalies and powered RFM segmentation. Claude-powered insights (with a rule-based fallback) and regex-based PII masking protected 1,983 sensitive records in a 20,000-row pipeline.
